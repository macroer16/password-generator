Make a password nobody can guess

Generated in your browser with its cryptographic random generator. Nothing you create or check here is saved or sent anywhere.

Options

Random words are easier to remember and type than random characters. Five words from this 7,776-word list is about 65 bits. Use six or more for important accounts.

How can this be safe?

Your browser turns the password into a SHA-1 hash and sends only the first 5 characters of that hash to the Have I Been Pwned Pwned Passwords service. The service sends back every leaked hash starting with those 5 characters, which is hundreds of them, and your browser checks the list itself. The password, and even its full hash, never leave this page.

Good habits

  • Use a different password for every account. A password manager remembers them for you.
  • Turn on multi-factor authentication for email first, because it can reset everything else.
  • Length beats complexity. A long passphrase is stronger than a short jumble.
